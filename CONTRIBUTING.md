# Contributing

Submit evidence, not credentials or marketing claims.

1. Open a Candidate issue or PR with a first-party URL, date checked, offer class, billing risk, region, and supported protocol.
2. CI checks schema, URL scheme, expiry, and obvious secrets.
3. An AI review may summarize evidence and flag gaps; it cannot mark an offer verified.
4. Two maintainers review `verified` entries. Missing or expired evidence becomes `stale`.
5. A vendor may claim an entry with a company-domain email or DNS TXT proof. Claiming does not bypass review.
